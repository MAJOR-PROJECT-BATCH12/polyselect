    How PolySelect works

    1. Identify

      An image model looks at your photo and names the plastic. If you already know it, choose it and skip this step.

    2. Recommend

      A second model ranks enzymes for that plastic and predicts the temperature, pH and time that work best.

    3. Support

      Each recommendation links to published studies, so you can check the evidence before you plan an experiment.

    PolySelect helps you plan experiments. Always confirm a recommendation in the lab before relying on it.
